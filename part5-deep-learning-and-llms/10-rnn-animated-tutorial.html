Opening: what are we trying to do?

We show a sequence one word at a time and ask the network to predict what comes next.

Let’s build one of the simplest language models ever created: the Recurrent Neural Network, or RNN.

input / embeddings hidden memory output prediction loss / learning

This interactive chapter is a companion to Chapter 10 — Recurrent Neural Networks. It uses a live 3D scene to make the RNN data flow physical: words become token IDs, IDs become embeddings, embeddings enter a recurrent cell, hidden state carries memory, logits become probabilities, and loss flows backward through time during training.

Use the scene slider for manual review. Generate MP3 narration with scripts/generate-rnn-narration-elevenlabs.mjs, then turn narration on to sync the animation to recorded audio. Captions remain available when audio files are not present.
